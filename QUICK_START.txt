## Quick Start (3 Steps)

### 1. Start Server
```
python app.py
```

### 2. Open Browser
Visit: http://localhost:5000

### 3. Upload & Analyze
- Click upload area or drag-and-drop video
- Select MP4, AVI, MOV, MKV, FLV, WMV, or WebM
- Max size: 500MB
- Click "Upload & Analyze"

---

## What Gets Detected

Violence Types:
- Mob violence
- Physical assault  
- Threatening gestures
- Fighting
- Weapons (knife, gun)
- Blood presence
- Smoke/explosions

Confidence Score:
- 0-30%: Low violence probability
- 30-50%: Moderate indicators
- 50-70%: High likelihood
- 70-100%: Strong detection

---

## Results Display

After upload, you'll see:

1. Overall Violence Confidence (%)
2. Violence/No Violence Classification
3. Detected Violence Indicators with scores
4. Extracted Frame Previews
5. Interactive Video Player with:
   - Violence Detection Timeline
   - Frame-by-frame Analysis
   - Current Frame & Time Display

---

## Video Timeline

The timeline shows violence detection across the video:
- Red: High confidence (>70%)
- Orange: Medium confidence (50-70%)
- Yellow: Low confidence (30-50%)

## Troubleshooting

Video upload fails:
- Check file format
- Verify size < 500MB
- Ensure video is readable

Model won't load:
- Check ckpt/wsanodet_mix2.pkl exists
- Verify PyTorch installation

High CPU usage:
- Process one video at a time
- Use smaller test videos

Video player not showing:
- Check browser console for errors (F12)
- Verify /uploads/ directory exists
- Check browser compatibility
